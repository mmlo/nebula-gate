Flight brief

HOW TO FLY

  • Move with WASD or arrows. A mouse inside the corridor flies the ship. A gamepad stick works too.
  • Hold Shift (or Focus) to slow down and show the hitbox. The red dot is death. The ring around it is the graze band.
  • Bullets are brighter than their dangerous core. Skim them to fill the bomb meter and build a combo.
  • Press X when the meter is full. A bomb clears enemy fire, damages everything, and pulls in score gems.
  • Gems drift toward you when you are close, when you fly high, or after a bomb.
  • You start with one bomb, five hulls, and two continues. Clear three waves, then break dreadnought Vesper.
